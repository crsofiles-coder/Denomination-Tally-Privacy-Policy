## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

All data you enter or configure in **Denomination Tally** is stored **exclusively on your device** using local app storage:

- **Cash Count Sessions & History:** Session titles, notes, currency codes, bill/coin quantities, and timestamps.
- **App Preferences:** Selected currency, thousands separator preference, theme mode (System / Light / Dark), Always-On screen preference, and custom or hidden denominations.
- **Local Device Label:** A randomly generated local identifier (e.g., `dev-xxxxxxxx`) created on-device to label your saved sessions and exported reports. This identifier is not tied to your name, email, phone number, or hardware serial numbers.

We do **not** have access to any of this data.

---

## 3. Data Collection, Analytics, and Third-Party Services

**Denomination Tally** does **not** include:
- User accounts or authentication
- Cloud storage or remote servers
- Analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
- Advertising or tracking SDKs

No network requests are made by the app to transmit your cash counts or usage behavior.

## 4. Exports (PDF & Excel) and Printing

When you choose to preview, print, or export a report (`.pdf` or `.xlsx`):
- The file is generated **locally on your device**.
- Files are only shared or sent outside the app when you explicitly initiate an action through your device's system print dialog or share sheet (for example, saving to Files, emailing, or messaging).

---

## 5. Device Features

- **Keep Screen On (Always On Toggle):** When you enable the **Always On** toggle in the app bar, the app uses the device wake-lock capability solely to prevent the screen from dimming or sleeping while you count cash. You can turn it off at any time.

---

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device:
- You can delete individual saved sessions in the **History** tab or remove custom denominations in **Settings** at any time.
- Uninstalling **Denomination Tally** or clearing the app's storage in your device settings permanently deletes all locally stored data.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

**Denomination Tally** does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).
